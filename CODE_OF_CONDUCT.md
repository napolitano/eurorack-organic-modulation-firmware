# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is intended to be a constructive, technically focused place for collaboration. Contributors, users and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith where reasonable and keep discussion centered on the project.

Useful participation includes specific technical feedback, disagreement with ideas rather than people, acknowledging and correcting mistakes, respecting different experience levels, and keeping issues/reviews relevant. Harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading remarks, personal attacks, sustained disruption or publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository and project-controlled communication spaces, including situations where somebody represents the project elsewhere. Technical criticism may be direct; it must remain criticism of code, mathematics, hardware behavior, documentation, testing or design decisions rather than personal abuse.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. Conduct concerns should be reported privately through a contact route published by the maintainer. Do not place sensitive incident details in a public issue.

Reports are handled on a best-effort basis and as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against good-faith reporting is itself unacceptable conduct.

## Reference

This project follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 model for situations not addressed explicitly here: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
